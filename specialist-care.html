SPECIALIZED TREATMENTS

Care tailored to your neurological needs

Specialist care may involve symptom review, diagnostic assessment, medication discussions, lifestyle guidance, and coordinated follow-up. The right approach depends on your symptoms, history, and clinical evaluation.

Headache & migraine planningSymptom and trigger review with treatment discussions.
Seizure evaluationAssessment and discussion of EEG or other investigations when indicated.
Nerve & neuromuscular concernsEvaluation of symptoms such as tingling, weakness, or nerve pain.
WHEN TO SEEK HELP

When should you see a neurologist?

A specialist assessment may help when symptoms are persistent, recurring, unexplained, or affecting everyday life.

Recurring symptoms

Headaches, migraines, dizziness, or sensory changes that keep returning.

Seizure concerns

Episodes or symptoms that need careful evaluation and follow-up.

Nerve-related changes

Persistent numbness, tingling, weakness, or nerve pain that needs assessment.

Seek emergency medical help for sudden weakness, facial drooping, difficulty speaking, a sudden severe headache, or other possible stroke symptoms.
